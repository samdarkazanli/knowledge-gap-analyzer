Application: payments
Responsible team: Payments Platform Team

Ownership boundary:
The Payments Platform Team owns operation and maintenance of the payments
application. The Elasticsearch Platform Team owns the shared Elasticsearch
service; it does not own payments application behavior or business logic.

Escalation responsibilities:
Operational issues affecting payments application behavior are escalated first
to the Payments Platform Team. Issues confirmed to originate in the shared
Elasticsearch service are then escalated by Payments Platform to the
Elasticsearch Platform Team with incident context and diagnostic evidence.

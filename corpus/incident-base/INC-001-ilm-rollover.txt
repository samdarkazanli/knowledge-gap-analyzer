Incident: INC-001
Date: 2026-06-14
System: Elasticsearch
Application: payments

The payments index stopped rolling over.

ILM remained in the check-rollover-ready step.

Investigation found that the alias payments-write existed,
but the current payments index was not configured as the
write index.

Setting is_write_index=true on the appropriate alias/index
relationship restored rollover operation.

Incident resolved at 16:42.
